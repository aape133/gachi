An ASIO audio device is needed

To keep latency low, gachi sends and receives sound through ASIO only. No ASIO driver was found on this PC.

Install the ASIO driver from your audio interface maker's website, then check again. If you use a DAW, choose VST link in Settings → Audio, and the app needs no ASIO device of its own.

    Input channel

    Make a sound and a bar rises on the channel the signal comes in on. With 'Mic + stereo' you can send your mic and a loopback (PC sound) together. Don't pick a loopback whose bar rises with the test tone: that channel carries this app's output (the others' sound), so they would hear themselves back.

    Sound quality

    Advanced
    Packet size

    Auto sends one packet per device buffer (split evenly above 64). Picking by hand, smaller is faster but means more upload packets, and going below the device buffer doesn't make it faster.

    Jitter buffer

    What to favor when the connection stalls now and then. 'Fast' keeps the buffer small and fills the stalls by patching the sound (latency ↓, short gaps ↑). 'Steady' grows the buffer to prevent them (latency ↑, gaps ↓). On an even connection all three are the same.

    Auto keeps each person at the smallest size without dropouts. You can also press the latency number on someone's card to adjust theirs.

    Turn this on when your router has no UPnP or it's off. On the router's admin page, forward this UDP port to this PC. Other PCs on the same router need different numbers. Applies from the next room you join.

    Connection status
    Language

    Draws the window with the graphics card. If the screen flickers or breaks up, turn it off. Sound and encryption (AES-256) are not affected. Applies after a restart.

    Applies after a restart.

    At start, the app asks GitHub once whether a new version is out. If there is one, the home screen only says so; nothing is downloaded or installed by itself. While asking, GitHub can see your internet address (IP).

    Turn on only when looking into a problem. Off, the log keeps brief events; on, it records the connection in detail every 2 seconds. Logs stay on this PC (the last 5) and are never sent anywhere. Names, chat and passwords are never logged.

    Privacy

    Sound and chat go encrypted directly (P2P) between the people in a room, never through a server. The connection server only introduces rooms and doesn't keep who connected (internet addresses included). While connecting, though, the connection server, its operator and Cloudflare, which hosts it, can see your internet address (IP). And since the connection is direct, people in the same room can find each other's IP.
