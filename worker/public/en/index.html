P2P jam app · Windows

Miles apart,
jam together

gachi is an app for jamming with others over the internet. Your sound goes straight to each other, never through a server. The app, the plugin and the connection server are all free and open source.

Get started

Start in three steps

  1. 01

    Install

    Run the installer, then pick your audio interface in Settings.

  2. 02

    Create a room

    Create a room and send the code or invite link to the people you play with.

  3. 03

    Play together

    When your friends join with the code, you hear each other right away.

What you need

  • Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  • An audio interface with an ASIO driver (with a DAW, the DAW handles the device)
  • A wired connection is recommended. Bigger rooms need plenty of upload speed.

FAQ

FAQ

How much delay is there?

It depends on distance, your internet and your audio devices. Nearby, like within the same city, on wired internet, it's short enough to hear each other and play in time. The app splits each person's delay into their device, the network, the buffer and your own device, so you can see what to improve.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, and all the source code is public.

I can't connect

gachi connects you directly, without a relay server. If both of you are on mobile hotspots, or on a network that blocks direct connections, like at work or school, it may not connect. The app then tells you why and what to try. If your router has no UPnP, forward the fixed port from the app's settings on your router.

How is my privacy handled?

Sound and chat travel encrypted and never pass through the server. The connection server only introduces you at the start and doesn't keep a record of who connected.

The app never shows other people's IP addresses, and its troubleshooting log keeps only part of an address. Still, to send sound straight to each other, the computers need each other's address, so someone using network analysis tools could find the IP addresses of people in the same room. Keep this in mind when playing with people you don't know.
